Merge Sort vs Quick Sort
Feature	 |   Merge Sort   |  	Quick Sort
Basic idea | Divide array into 2 halves, sort them, merge |	Choose pivot, partition around pivot
Technique |	Divide & Conquer |	Divide & Conquer
Best case	O(n log n)	O(n log n)
Average case	O(n log n)	O(n log n)
Worst case	O(n log n)	O(n²)
Extra space	O(n) for arrays	O(log n) average due to recursion
Stable?	✅ Yes	❌ Usually no
In-place?	❌ Usually no	✅ Yes
Performance on arrays	Good	Often faster in practice
Linked lists	⭐ Excellent	Usually less convenient
Worst-case guarantee	✅ Yes	❌ No, unless carefully designed
Main operation	Merge	Partition
